## ETFsChecklist

### Check list for ETFs

NSE -> Traded Volume (lakhs) -> 51.40

- Daily average volume (example more than 5 Cr)
- ETF AUM ABOVE 500 CRS
- EXPENSE RATIO
- TRACKING ERROR should be low
- i-NAV (compare iNAV vs Stock price)
- BID Vs ASK Price
